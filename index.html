      The Chaldean Number Chart

      In the Chaldean system, letters are assigned values 1–8. The number 9 is considered sacred and is never assigned to a letter.

      Note: values are based on the sound & vibration of each letter, which is why Chaldean numerology differs from the Pythagorean (Western) system.
